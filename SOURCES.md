# Folioheart content sources — v1.5.0

Character options only; no official artwork or book scans are bundled. Existing core IDs and storage keys are preserved.

## Existing core catalogue

Core SRD 1.0, structured by [daggersearch/daggerheart-data](https://github.com/daggersearch/daggerheart-data). The 189 domain cards, nine classes, 18 subclasses, 18 ancestries and nine communities retain their existing text and identifiers.

## Hope & Fear — released rules

[Official SRD page](https://www.daggerheart.com/srd/). [SRD 2.0 PDF](https://www.daggerheart.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/DH_SRD_2_2026_08_25.pdf), August 25, 2026. Added four classes, eight subclasses, 21 Dread cards, six ancestries, six communities and six transformations. Text is reformatted for cards and screens. Martial Artist stance rules are included as reference.

## The Void — current playtest

[Official Void hub](https://www.daggerheart.com/thevoid/). Both packets are dated July 9, 2026.

- [Summoner](https://www.daggerheart.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Summoner_Void_2026_07_09.pdf): Summoner, Necromancy and Theurgy.
- [Blood Hunter](https://www.daggerheart.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Blood_Hunter_Void_2026_07_09.pdf): Blood Hunter, Order of the Specter, Order of the Mutant, Order of the Lycan, and the 21 Blood domain cards shared with Summoner.

Retired Void playtests for Assassin, Brawler, Warlock and Witch are not used in place of their released Hope & Fear rules.

## Homebrew

The app supplies an editor; it does not bundle third-party subclass text. Enter your own campaign rules. Sample subclass text in automated tests is test data and is never included as selectable content.
